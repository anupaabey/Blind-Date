# Before a live launch

- [ ] Review the actual operating entity, business address, support process, Sri Lankan PDPA obligations and relevant amendments/commencement, cross-border data processing, consumer terms, privacy notices and refund terms with qualified Sri Lankan counsel. Replace all legal drafts with reviewed documents and version consent accordingly.
- [ ] Use separate staging and production Supabase projects. Apply all migrations, verify RLS/grants and constraints, disable demo seeding, preserve offsite backups and test restore/deletion/retention procedures.
- [ ] Verify production Auth signup, actual confirmation and password-recovery emails, SMTP sender authentication, strong passwords, sessions, CAPTCHA/rate limits and account deletion. Require administrator MFA and maintain least-privilege staff access and periodic role reviews.
- [ ] Establish a proportionate Sri Lankan residency review procedure, an adult/safety review process, appeals and operational staffing. Do not infer residency, nationality or ethnicity from phone numbers, IPs, names or language. Do not collect unnecessary identity documents.
- [ ] Contract and vet real public Colombo venues, record accurate locations and contacts, agree costs and cancellation terms, and confirm each reservation manually. Never activate fictional venues for genuine members.
- [ ] Obtain merchant approval for the service. Validate actual PayHere sandbox checkout, signed notifications, amount/currency/merchant mismatch, duplicate callbacks, failures, cancellation, receipts, chargebacks and reconciliation. Process a genuine sandbox refund where supported and review how real refunds are executed and recorded. Do not enable live payments until all financial/consumer requirements are satisfied.
- [ ] Configure email/SMS providers only where consent and credentials exist. Verify actual delivery and opt-in/transactional purposes. Adjust cron to operational needs, monitor/retry the outbox and route incidents to staffed support. Do not claim that local Mailpit capture establishes real deliverability.
- [ ] Test the full flow with two separate accounts, administrative review, one-sided acceptance, rejected/expired invitations, changed preferences, blocking/suspension, cancellations/rescheduling, payment, date completion, private feedback and mutual contact consent. Run unit, real database, browser and production-build checks on the exact release.
- [ ] Review accessibility with keyboard and assistive technology, mobile devices, dark mode, motion preferences, Sri Lankan locale/date/phone handling and missing towns. Collect genuine testimonial consent; remove all demonstrative quotes from marketing if they could be mistaken for real member stories.
- [ ] Configure HTTPS/domain/DNS, network destinations, server-only secrets, monitoring, dependency scanning, alerts, log redaction, cache rules, HSTS/CSP and a security incident response plan. Establish safety-report escalation and actual emergency contacts. Review abuse defenses at the edge.
- [ ] Deploy to staging, verify the real hosted integrations, review release changes, then deliberately publish to production. A GitHub repository or cloud environment snapshot is not a Vercel deployment.
